Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How do I play?

  • Hold GAS to accelerate and BRAKE to slow down or reverse, and balance the car on slopes
  • Collect coins, gems and fuel cans; the run ends when you run out of fuel
  • Spend coins in TUNE to upgrade your car, and use GARAGE to change vehicle, paint and area

2. Does the game need an internet connection?

  • No, you can play fully offline
  • A connection is only needed to load ads or process purchases

3. How do rewarded ads work?

  • You can choose to watch an ad to revive, double your coins after a run, or earn free coins in the SHOP
  • If no ad is ready, please try again in a few minutes

4. How do I remove ads / restore purchases?

  • Buy Remove Ads or the Starter Pack in the SHOP
  • After changing devices or reinstalling, use the restore-purchases button in the SHOP with the same Apple/Google account

5. I lost my coins/gems after reinstalling?

  • Game data is stored only on your device, so uninstalling erases progress and it cannot be restored
  • Non-consumable purchases can be restored; coins and gems cannot

6. Does the game collect personal information?

  • No account is required and progress stays on your device; ads served by Google AdMob may use an advertising identifier according to your consent choice
  • See the Privacy Policy
