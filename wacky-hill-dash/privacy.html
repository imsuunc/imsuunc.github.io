🔒 Privacy Policy

Wacky Hill Dash - Hill-Climb Driving Game

Last updated: Oct 3, 2026

Wacky Hill Dash (the "App", "we") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and how you stay in control.

1. Information we collect

The App does not require an account. Your progress (coins, gems, upgrades, vehicles, paints, distance records, daily-reward streak) is stored only on your device and is never uploaded to our servers.

1.1 Data you create while playing

  • Coin and gem balances, vehicle upgrade levels
  • Unlocked vehicles and paints, and the selected area
  • Distance records, daily-reward streak, and whether Remove Ads / Starter Pack has been purchased

This data is stored locally (SharedPreferences) and is not sent anywhere.

1.2 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows banner ads, interstitial ads (after a few runs) and rewarded ads (watch to revive, double coins or earn free coins) through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device advertising identifier, IP address, device information and ad-interaction data to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. Where required (for example the EEA and the UK), the App shows Google's consent form (UMP) before loading ads; on iOS you may also be asked for App Tracking Transparency permission. If you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalised. You can change your choice in your device settings. See how Google uses data in advertising.

2. Device permissions

The App does not access your contacts, location, camera, photo library or microphone, and does not request notification permission.

3. In-app purchases

Wacky Hill Dash is free to play. The App offers optional purchases:

  • Remove Ads and Starter Pack (one-time, non-renewing)
  • Coin packs and gem packs (consumable in-game items with no real-world cash value)
  • Payments are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play; we never see or store your card or payment details
  • Pricing, cancellations and refunds follow Apple/Google policies — see the Terms of Use

4. Third-party services

Wacky Hill Dash uses only the following third-party services, strictly for the purposes below:

Service Purpose
Google AdMob / Google UMP Serving ads and the privacy consent form
Apple App Store / Google Play Billing Processing in-app purchase payments

We do not sell user data to third parties.

5. Children's privacy

Wacky Hill Dash is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us so we can address it.

6. Your rights

Because your game data lives only on your device, you stay in control:

  • Delete all game data by uninstalling the App
  • Change your ad/tracking choices anytime in your device settings (for example Privacy & Security > Tracking on iOS, or Google ad settings on Android)
  • Contact us at the email below with any privacy question

Because we do not store data on a server, we cannot restore progress lost when you uninstall the App or change devices. Purchases can be restored through your Apple/Google account.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time; the latest version is always posted on this page.
