About the Game

Wacky Hill Dash is a fun hill-climb driving game: use gas and brake to keep your balance over bumpy slopes, grab coins and gems, and dodge springs, rolling rocks, birds, sinkholes, lightning and falling power lines. When the fuel runs out the run is over — go as far as you can, then spend your coins on engine, fuel tank, tires, brakes, suspension, magnet and bounty upgrades. Progress is saved right on your device — no account needed.
